# Sources — The World Today, 22 November 2026

Every story on the front page was verified against a source before it
was drawn. The rule applied: a story counts for 22 November when it
happened on that day or was first reported on 22 November 2026 in
some time zone.

## The seven stories

1. **Gaza — Four nations demand aid access (lead).**
   Australia, Canada, Italy and the United Kingdom jointly called on
   Israel on Sunday to grant immediate, unhindered humanitarian access
   across Gaza, saying recent flooding has added to the suffering;
   about 150,000 tents and other essential items remain stuck at the
   border. The UN had put those affected by the flooding at about
   226,000 people, with more than 40,000 shelters damaged.
   Source: The Paris Eye, 22 Nov 2026 —
   https://thepariseye.com/breaking-news
   and https://nexuseuropa.eu/en/story/severe-winter-storms-exacerbate-humanitarian-crisis-in-gaza-as-western-nations-u-27473
   and https://skardutimes.com/news/gaza-floods-damage-40000-shelters-as-eu-presses-israel-on-aid-713e7a

2. **The Gulf — Saudi defences down Houthi missile near Mecca.**
   The Saudi-led coalition said its air defences intercepted and
   destroyed a ballistic missile launched by Yemen's Houthi movement
   toward western Saudi Arabia in the early hours of Sunday; civil
   defence had warned the Mecca region about two hours earlier.
   Source: Anadolu Agency via House of Saud, 22 Nov 2026 —
   https://houseofsaud.com/houthi-missile-mecca-iran-bahrain-fire-nov22/
   and https://cryptobriefing.com/saudi-coalition-intercepts-houthi-missile-targeting-western-saudi-arabia/

3. **Climate — COP31 talks resume, still deadlocked.**
   Negotiators at the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, were
   due to resume talks on Sunday after running into overtime with no
   deal; the draft text drops fossil-fuel references and a $120bn
   adaptation-finance target, and the EU's top climate official said
   it would be "a good deal or no deal".
   Source: Reuters, 21 Nov 2026 —
   https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/cop31-climate-summit-risks-failure-talks-go-deep-into-overtime-2026-11-21/
   and https://www.europesays.com/australia/151171/ (talks resume Sunday, 22 Nov)

4. **Israel — Eisenkot opens unity talks with Likud.**
   Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming Israel's next government, invited
   Benjamin Netanyahu's Likud party to coalition talks, aiming for a
   national unity government; talks were scheduled to begin on Sunday,
   22 November.
   Source: Daily Jagran, 22 Nov 2026 —
   https://www.thedailyjagran.com/world/news/israel-government-formation-arab-parties-snubbed-as-gadi-eisenkot-reaches-out-to-benjamin-netanyahus-likud-in-shocking-coalition-move-10337188
   and https://www.europesays.com/3391643/

5. **Myanmar — Magnitude 4.1 quake before dawn.**
   An earthquake of magnitude 4.1 struck Myanmar in the early hours
   of Sunday, at 02:35:57 IST (03:35 local time), at a depth of 95 km,
   according to India's National Centre for Seismology.
   Source: ANI via New Kerala, 22 Nov 2026 —
   https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/earthquake-magnitude-41-hits-myanmar-917.htm
   and https://www.dailyprabhat.com/earthquake-of-magnitude-4-1-hits-myanmar/

6. **Sport — Zverev and Alcaraz meet for the Turin crown.**
   World No. 1 Alexander Zverev, unbeaten all week, faces Carlos
   Alcaraz in the Nitto ATP Finals title match in Turin on Sunday
   evening (not before 6 p.m. CET); Zverev seeks a third Finals title,
   Alcaraz his first, on a 13-match winning streak.
   Source: ATP Tour, 21–22 Nov 2026 —
   http://www.atptour.com/en/news/nitto-atp-finals-2026-final-preview
   and http://www.atptour.com/en/news/nitto-atp-finals-2026-final-schedule

7. **Hong Kong — Voting begins for the Election Committee.**
   Hong Kong opened polling stations on Sunday for about 5,700 voters
   to elect new members of the Election Committee, the body that helps
   choose the city's leader.
   Source: Global Digest, 22 Nov 2026 —
   https://news.cryptoeconomics.tech/geopolitics-22-11-2026-0147/
